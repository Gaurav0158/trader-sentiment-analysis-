# trader-sentiment-analysis-
Data analysis project exploring trader profitability and behavior during Fear vs Greed market conditions.

Key Insights
Insight 1 — Extreme Greed days produced the highest profitability

Average Closed PnL:

Sentiment	Avg PnL
Extreme Greed	67.89
Fear	54.29
Greed	42.74
Extreme Fear	34.53
Neutral	34.30
Interpretation
Traders earned the highest profits during Extreme Greed periods.
Market momentum and bullish sentiment likely increased successful directional trades.
Neutral and Extreme Fear periods showed lower profitability, indicating uncertainty and defensive market behavior.
Strong write-up line

Trader profitability increased significantly during Extreme Greed periods, suggesting that bullish market sentiment positively influenced short-term trading outcomes.

Insight 2 — Traders became more aggressive during Fear periods

Average Trade Size:

Sentiment	Avg Trade Size (USD)
Fear	7816
Greed	5736
Extreme Fear	5349
Neutral	4782
Extreme Greed	3112
Interpretation
Surprisingly, traders used the largest position sizes during Fear periods.
This may indicate:
aggressive dip-buying
risk-taking under volatility
attempts to recover losses
Important observation

Even though Fear periods had large trade sizes, profitability did not become highest.

Strong write-up

Traders increased average position sizes significantly during Fear periods, indicating more aggressive exposure despite uncertain market conditions.

Insight 3 — Win rate was highest during Extreme Greed

Win Rate:

Sentiment	Win Rate
Extreme Greed	46.49%
Fear	42.07%
Neutral	39.69%
Greed	38.48%
Extreme Fear	37.06%
Interpretation
Extreme Greed markets improved trade success probability.
Extreme Fear produced the lowest win rates, suggesting unstable market conditions.
Strong write-up

Trade success probability improved during bullish sentiment periods, while Extreme Fear conditions resulted in the weakest trading consistency.

Insight 5 — SELL bias increased during Extreme Greed

Direction Distribution:

Sentiment	BUY	SELL
Extreme Greed	44.85%	55.14%
Interpretation
Traders showed stronger SELL-side preference during Extreme Greed.
Possible explanation:
profit booking
short-term reversal expectations
Strong write-up

Traders displayed a stronger sell-side bias during Extreme Greed conditions, potentially indicating profit-taking behavior near market highs.

Insight 6 — Infrequent traders outperformed frequent traders
Segment	Avg PnL
Frequent Traders	42.49
Infrequent Traders	96.94
Interpretation
Overtrading may reduce profitability.
Less frequent traders likely took higher quality setups.
Strong write-up

Infrequent traders generated substantially higher average profitability, suggesting that selective trade execution outperformed excessive market participation.

Insight 7 — Large trades generated much higher PnL
Trade Size	Avg PnL
Low Size	4.38
High Size	93.11
Interpretation
High exposure trades produced significantly larger returns.
However, likely accompanied by higher volatility/risk.
Strong write-up

Larger position sizes were associated with significantly higher profitability, although such strategies may also expose traders to elevated downside volatility.
